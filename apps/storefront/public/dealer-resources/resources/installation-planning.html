Installation planning checklist

A consistent handoff from the sales counter to the installation bay.

Confirm the job

Record the vehicle year, make, model, trim, factory audio package, and the customer’s priorities. Confirm fitment against the product and vehicle documentation before ordering.

Build the parts list

List the main components, vehicle-specific interfaces, mounting hardware, and required accessories. Include quantities and the job reference so the receiving team can match deliveries to the work order.

Plan the installation

Review the manufacturer’s instructions, vehicle service information, and any calibration or programming requirements. Verify that the shop has the appropriate tools and qualified personnel.

Check the completed work

Use the manufacturer’s commissioning procedure. Confirm retained vehicle functions, inspect workmanship, and document any settings or changes for the customer.

Complete the handoff

Give the customer operating instructions and warranty documentation. Record the installed part numbers and serial numbers, where applicable, with the work order.

Example dealer resource for the Velocity demonstration environment. Fictional businesses; no products available for purchase or fulfillment. This guide does not replace manufacturer instructions or vehicle service documentation.
