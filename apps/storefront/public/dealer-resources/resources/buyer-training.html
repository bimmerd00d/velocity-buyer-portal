A new buyer’s first order

A short walkthrough of the dealer purchasing workflow.

Start with your company

Check the company shown in the workspace. Confirm that the shipping address and your purchasing permissions are appropriate for the order.

Find and organize products

Search by part number or browse the catalog. Use a shopping list to group products for a customer installation or a future stock order.

Review purchasing details

Check quantities, account pricing, availability, and any required accessories. Add a purchase order or job reference so the order can be reconciled later.

Track the work

Use My orders for your purchases and Company orders for the wider account activity when your role allows it. Open a quote to review its current terms before accepting it.

Review invoices

Use Invoices to check open amounts, due dates, and payment history. In this preview, the remaining credit figure is an illustrative calculation, not checkout authorization.

Example dealer resource for the Velocity demonstration environment. Fictional businesses; no products available for purchase or fulfillment. This guide does not replace manufacturer instructions or vehicle service documentation.
