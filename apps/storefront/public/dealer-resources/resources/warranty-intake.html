Warranty & returns intake

Gather the details that help a service team review a request.

Identify the purchase

Record the invoice number, order date, part number, quantity, and serial number. Keep proof of purchase with the case.

Describe the issue

Write down the reported symptom, when it occurs, and the troubleshooting already performed. Attach clear photographs of the product and packaging where relevant.

Check the applicable policy

Review the manufacturer’s warranty and the seller’s return terms. Coverage, deadlines, exclusions, and approval requirements vary; this checklist does not promise coverage.

Request authorization

Submit the information to the appropriate service contact. Wait for authorization and shipping instructions before sending goods.

Prepare the return

Follow the approved packing instructions and keep tracking information with the case. Record the final resolution for the customer.

Example dealer resource for the Velocity demonstration environment. Fictional businesses; no products available for purchase or fulfillment. This guide does not replace manufacturer instructions or vehicle service documentation.
