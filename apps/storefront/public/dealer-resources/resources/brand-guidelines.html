Velocity Dealer brand essentials

A practical reference for dealer presentations and collateral.

Use the supplied artwork

Use the Velocity Dealer SVG supplied in the brand kit. Retain the artwork’s proportions, lettering, and colors. Avoid rebuilding the wordmark with a substitute font.

Give the logo room

Leave clear space around the logo and keep it away from busy imagery. The supplied light logo is intended for dark backgrounds.

Match the account palette

Navy #14212B anchors headers. Cyan #83DCE5 highlights interactive states. Paper #F3F5F5 supports the workspace. Ink #18242E is the primary text color.

Keep the page legible

Use Barlow for interface text and Barlow Condensed for section headings. Maintain readable contrast and reserve accent color for meaningful emphasis.

Describe the environment accurately

Velocity storefronts and dealer companies are fictional businesses used for demonstrations. No products are available for purchase and no orders will be fulfilled.
